Madrid

I shoot fashion, musicians and live events, in studio light and in dark clubs.

Ukrainian photographer and videographer based in Madrid, with four years behind the camera. I have photographed Sviatoslav Vakarchuk, Odyn v Kanoe, Real Madrid goalkeeper Andriy Lunin and Kalush Orchestra, the Eurovision 2022 winners.

I photographed Tom Holland at a mini-premiere of Spider-Man: Brand New Day in Madrid, worked at film premieres attended by Oscar-winning directors, and at corporate events for Headway Inc and Beatmotiv.

I am also a musician. I study viola at the Real Conservatorio Superior de Música de Madrid and play violin, piano and guitar. On a concert stage I can read the score and feel where the peak is coming, so I am ready for it.

I edit in Lightroom and DaVinci Resolve and deliver both photo and video from the same shoot, in Madrid or wherever you are. At home my cat supervises every edit and is the toughest critic I have.
