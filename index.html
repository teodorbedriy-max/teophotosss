About me

Bio · Experience · Clients

Ukrainian photographer and videographer based in Madrid, with four years behind the camera.

I shoot fashion, musicians and live events. I have photographed Sviatoslav Vakarchuk, Odyn v Kanoe, Real Madrid goalkeeper Andriy Lunin and Kalush Orchestra, the Eurovision 2022 winners. I photographed Tom Holland at a mini-premiere of Spider-Man: Brand New Day in Madrid, at the opening of the football World Cup. I have worked at film premieres attended by Oscar-winning directors, and at corporate events for Headway Inc and Beatmotiv.

I am also a musician. I study viola at the Real Conservatorio Superior de Música de Madrid and play violin, piano and guitar, and I sing. On a concert stage I can read the score and feel where the peak is coming, so I am ready for it.

I work in studio light as comfortably as in a dark club, edit in Lightroom and DaVinci Resolve, and deliver both photo and video from the same shoot.
